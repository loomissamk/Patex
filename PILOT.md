# Research pilot

Start with five invited mathematics/statistics/science researchers, using their own manuscripts and consent. The owner creates invitations privately in Account settings. No invitation email is sent automatically.

Each participant should import a complete project, obtain a PDF without rewriting its source, add a citation and plot, use the offline library, try an AI suggestion only if desired, restore a revision, export the source, and rebuild the export. Record the failing file/dependency and a redacted diagnostic when anything breaks. Never publish their manuscript as a test fixture without permission.

Measure time to first successful PDF, import/build success, editing/build latency, weekly completed sessions, and time saved compared with their existing workflow. Ask what blocks publication and what they would pay for; a completed PDF is more useful evidence than a feature vote. Retain source/PDF hashes and runtime identity with explicit participant consent.

Before charging: validate the subscription gate in TODO.md, agree a price and support terms with willing participants, configure a payment provider and cancellation workflow, and verify a restore from an off-host backup. Do not collect card details in Patex. Invite-only pilot accounts and a public source repository do not imply public signups or billing are live.
